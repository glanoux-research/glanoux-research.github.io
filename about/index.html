From network cybersecurity to physical systems and AI, my path connects infrastructure, simulation, and experimental research.

My journey

  1. 2026–2027 · Deep Learning Lab, Seoul National University

    Dual-degree studies at SNU in the Deep Learning Lab of the Department of Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering. My research interests include cybersecurity with machine learning, LLM and deep learning architecture optimization, and implementation on hardware and edge devices.

    Explore the lab

  2. Current research · maritime navigation

    A testbed linking a ship manoeuvring model, collision avoidance and attacks on the NMEA/AIS data path. The question: when does an automatic response to a navigation-data alarm make the ship safer, and when does it put it at risk?

  3. 2025 · network and system administration internship

    At Caisse de Prévoyance Sociale in Papeete: segmentation of the front-office network, equipment migration, server configuration, and network monitoring.

  4. 2025 · academic projects

    Development of an FPGA Ethernet card and a study of hardware acceleration for post-quantum cryptography at INSA Toulouse.

  5. 2023–2024 · university exchange

    Exchange semester at Université Laval in Québec City.

  6. Degree in progress · INSA Toulouse

    Engineering degree in computer science and networks in progress, specializing in network cybersecurity.
