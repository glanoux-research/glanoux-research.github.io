What made this work

Reward, tuned for speed not pace

  • Velocity-toward-target reward is linear and capped, not a fixed-pace Gaussian — going faster is always better, up to a stability limit
  • A flat per-step time cost, so finishing sooner strictly wins over dawdling
  • Heading/upright terms are zero-centered penalties, not free reward for existing — standing still nets roughly zero, never positive

Confirmed before training, not after

  • A reused flat-ground walker (which can't turn) scored worse than standing still here — correctly, since it walked straight past B
  • Checked the reward formula directly instead: standing < partial turn < aligned & fast, strictly increasing
  • Only trained on this fixed B/C layout — a randomized-waypoint mode exists but hasn't been trained on
