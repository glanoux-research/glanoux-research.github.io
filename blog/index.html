14 July 2026 · Sebitseom Floating Island, Seoul

Bastille Day 2026 in Seoul

France’s national day, celebrated in Seoul by the French-Korean Chamber of Commerce and Industry (FKCCI) with the French Embassy in Korea. This edition also marked the FKCCI’s 40th anniversary and 140 years of diplomatic relations between France and Korea. More than 700 guests on the floating island on the Han River: speeches by the FKCCI president and the French ambassador, networking with French and Korean companies, and fireworks over the river.

6 July 2026 · Oakwood Premier Coex Center, Seoul

Trustworthy & Responsible Agentic AI Symposium

A half-day symposium hosted by the Center for Trustworthy AI (CTAI) at Seoul National University. The topic: agentic AI, systems that plan, reason and act on their own over long horizons, and how to make them reliable, accountable and governable. Two sessions of invited talks from academia and industry, each followed by a discussion:

  • Human Agency in the Age of Agentic AI · S. Shyam Sundar, Penn State University
  • Market Dynamics in an Agentic AI Economy · Haksoo Ko, Seoul National University School of Law
  • Rethinking Rewards in Reinforcement Learning · Masashi Sugiyama, RIKEN AIP and University of Tokyo
  • From Safe Models to Safe Agents · Youchul Kim, LG AI Research

30 June 2026 · French Embassy in Korea, Seoul

Visit to the French Embassy in Korea

An official visit with my lab to the French Embassy in Seoul, to discuss university cooperation between France and Korea and the challenges it raises. As students who made the move between the two countries, we shared our own experience of student mobility between France and Korea.
