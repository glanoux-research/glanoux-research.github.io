Hello, I’m Gabriel Lanoux.

I build and run complex systems — ship simulations, learning robots, GPU compute — and I know how to secure them.

I am a dual-degree student in computer science and networks between INSA Toulouse and Seoul National University; my engineering degree is in progress. At SNU’s Deep Learning Lab, my thesis builds a ship-navigation testbed that measures what attacks on navigation data, and the automatic responses to them, actually do to the ship.
